### 一、开场 · 25年与那日的恐怖

**EN**　In 25 years, a generation has passed since the terror attacks of September 11, 2001, yet time has not dimmed the horror of that day when our country came face-to-face with true evil.

**中**　25年过去了，自2001年9月11日恐怖袭击以来，一代人已经走过；但时间并没有冲淡那一日的恐怖——那一天，我们的国家直面了真正的邪恶。

**EN**　With the events of that morning, 2,977 beautiful souls were ripped from our world, and many more died in the weeks and years after, horrible, grotesque injuries, so many were sustained.

**中**　随着那个清晨的事变，2,977个美丽的灵魂被从我们的世界夺走；而在此后的数周与数年里，又有更多人在可怕而狰狞的伤痛中离世，受伤者不计其数。

**EN**　Free people must never avert their eyes from such hideous cruelty. We cannot forget that moment of immense and horrible evil that took place in this world, bent on the destruction of all that is good.

**中**　自由之民绝不可对如此狰狞的残暴转开目光。我们绝不能忘记，这世上曾发生的那场巨大而可怕的邪恶——它一心要毁掉一切良善。

### 二、追忆 · 遇难者故事

**EN**　On board Flight 77 were three 11-year-olds on a cross-country field trip, a family on an international adventure, and a flight attendant who had just found out she was pregnant.

**中**　77号航班上有三名11岁的孩子，他们正踏上横跨全国的校外旅行；有一家人在进行跨国之旅；还有一名乘务员，刚刚得知自己怀了身孕。

**EN**　At the Pentagon was a U.S. Navy airman who had just hung up after a call with his 10-year-old son, while a secretary was celebrating her birthday. Shortly after, every single one of those people was murdered by terrorists.

**中**　在五角大楼里，一名美国海军航空兵刚刚挂断与10岁儿子的通话；一名秘书正在庆祝自己的生日。片刻之后，这些人无一例外，全被恐怖分子杀害。

**EN**　General James Jackson ordered up the largest flag in the military's collection. First responders climbed to the top of the walls and hung it next to the gaping holes in this building as a symbol of American defiance. This morning, that same flag is here to my right — for the first time since 2001, along with three firefighters who raised it.

**中**　詹姆斯·杰克逊将军调来了军方收藏中最大的一面国旗。急救人员攀上墙顶，将它悬挂在这栋建筑巨大的裂口旁，作为美国不屈的象征。今天早晨，这面同样的旗帜就在我的右手边——这是自2001年以来首次，与当年将它升起的三名消防员一同出现。

### 三、团结 · 「我们都是纽约人」

**EN**　In those harrowing days after the attacks, we saw the authentic spirit of our nation. We were all New Yorkers that day. Every one of us. We were all New Yorkers. We were all family.

**中**　在袭击之后那些令人惶恐的日子里，我们看到了这个国家最本真的精神。那一天，我们都是纽约人。我们每一个人。我们都是纽约人。我们都是一家人。

**EN**　September 11th reminded us of the terrible power of hate. The 25 years since have shown us the even greater power of the American spirit to endure and prevail, and to win.

**中**　9月11日让我们记起了仇恨那可怕的力量。而此后的25年，则向我们展示了美国精神更为强大的力量——忍耐、制胜，并且赢得胜利。

### 四、誓言 · 永不忘记

**EN**　Today, we renew the sacred oath that we first swore in their names a quarter of a century ago — we will never, ever forget.

**中**　今天，我们重申那25年前首次以他们之名立下的神圣誓言——我们永远不会、永远不会忘记。

**EN**　We will honor their lives. We will cherish their beautiful memories. And we will protect this country and its freedoms for every future generation.

**中**　我们将尊崇他们的生命。我们将珍视他们美好的记忆。我们将为一代又一代的后人，守护这个国家及其自由。

**EN**　No one can ever prepare for a day like 9/11, but we can always choose to be the kind of nation the world saw in those days after.

**中**　没有人能为一如9月11日这样的日子做好准备；但我们总能选择，成为那个在事后数日里为世界所见的民族。

### 五、关联 · 反恐与伊朗

**EN**　Today, we salute every member of the United States military who served in the war on terror. It is a war on sick, horrible terror. We salute our first responders and law enforcement and we love them. And we salute the service members working right now to ensure the world's number one sponsor of terror, the Islamic Republic of Iran, will never ever have a nuclear weapon.

**中**　今天，我们向每一位投身反恐战争的美国军人致敬。这是一场对抗病态、可怕之恐怖的战争。我们向急救人员和执法者致敬，我们爱他们。我们向此刻正在执行任务的军人致敬——他们正确保全世界头号恐怖主义赞助国、伊朗伊斯兰共和国，永远、永远无法拥有核武器。

**EN**　That's why we fight today. We don't have a choice. There can only be victory. We fight hard. We fight to win.

**中**　这正是我们今天战斗的原因。我们别无选择。只能胜利。我们全力战斗。我们为胜利而战。

### 六、英雄 · 教育与精神

**EN**　We can raise our children to know that their country is good, that their fathers are brave, and that our flag is a symbol of justice and freedom. And we can be a nation whose heroes are not fanatics for an abstract cause — but the kind who race into buildings and give their lives on a Tuesday morning for strangers they've never met.

**中**　我们可以教育我们的孩子：他们的国家是良善的，他们的父辈是勇敢的，我们的国旗是正义与自由的象征。我们可以成为一个这样的国家——它的英雄不是为某种抽象事业而狂热的人，而是那种在周二清晨冲进楼宇、为素不相识的陌生人献出生命的人。

### 七、考验 · 美国的质地

**EN**　On days like 9/11, we learn what a country is truly made of. We're learning right now what we're made of, and we have to be strong and we have to be smart.

**中**　在如9月11日这样的日子里，我们才看清一个国家究竟由什么铸就。此刻我们正在看清自己由什么铸就，而我们必须坚强，也必须明智。

**EN**　Americans are made of a goodness and spirit that can transcend any earthly force. We're being tested today, and we're winning that test very handily. Faith is still believing in that future, even after you have seen the absolute worst. Innocence is expecting the future to be good because you have not yet really been tested. We've been tested many times.

**中**　美国人由一种良善与精神所铸就，那能超越任何世俗的力量。今天，我们正经受考验，而且我们正以极大的优势赢得这场考验。信仰，是在你见过最彻骨的至恶之后，依然相信那个未来。天真，是因为尚未真正受过考验，便期待未来美好。而我们，已被考验过许多次。

### 八、结尾 · 更强大，且长存

**EN**　Today our country is stronger than it has ever been before. It has never been as strong as it is right now. Because of that spirit, when future generations come here in 25, 100 or 250 years from now, they will stand in a nation that is free and unyielding in the face of evil. And they will still honor the heroes of 9/11/2001 — who no age will erase from the memory of time.

**中**　今天，我们的国家比以往任何时候都更强大。它从未像此刻这般强大。正因为那种精神，当25年、100年或250年后的后人来到这里，他们将站在一个自由、在邪恶面前不屈的国家里。而他们仍会尊崇2001年9月11日的英雄们——任何时代都无法将他们从时间的记忆中抹去。

**EN**　Twenty-five years ago, our Nation changed forever. But September 11 is remembered not only for what we lost, but for what we found: unity, love, courage, and an unshakable patriotism. God bless America.

**中**　25年前，我们的国家永远地改变了。但9月11日为人所铭记，不单是因为我们失去了什么，更是因为我们找到了什么：团结、爱、勇气，以及坚定不移的爱国心。愿上帝保佑美国。

### 附录 · 国防部长赫格塞思同期发言（节选）

**EN**　September 11, 2001, was one of the darkest days in American history. But evil did not have the final word. And it never will. The struggle against evil continued, and it continues now. It will continue until judgment day. Our only answer here on Earth is eternal vigilance.

**中**　2001年9月11日，是美国历史上最黑暗的日子之一。但邪恶并未写下终章。它也永不会。对抗邪恶的斗争延续着，此刻仍在延续。它将一直延续到审判之日。我们在尘世唯一的回应，是永恒的警惕。
