    <h2>二、韦伯：接棒后，看得更远 <span class="en-title">II. Webb: Receiving the Torch, Seeing Farther</span></h2>



    <div class="bilingual-block cn">

        <div class="lang-label">中文</div>

        <p>哈勃的主要缺点是观测波长偏短——它最擅长可见光和紫外，对遥远星系因宇宙膨胀而红移的光线无能为力。为此，NASA启动了「下一代太空望远镜」项目，后命名为詹姆斯·韦伯空间望远镜。</p>

    </div>

    <div class="bilingual-block en">

        <div class="lang-label">English</div>

        <p>Hubble's key limitation was its wavelength range—it excelled in visible and ultraviolet light but was powerless against the redshifted light from distant galaxies, stretched by cosmic expansion. To address this, NASA launched the <strong>Next Generation Space Telescope</strong> project, later named the James Webb Space Telescope.</p>

    <h2>三、罗曼：更大视野，更深秘密 <span class="en-title">III. Roman: Wider View, Deeper Secrets</span></h2>



    <div class="bilingual-block cn">

        <div class="lang-label">中文</div>

        <p>就在今天，2026年8月30日，罗曼太空望远镜搭乘猎鹰重型火箭正式升空。如果说哈勃是「精密的显微镜」，韦伯是「超远望远镜」，那罗曼就是「广角全景相机」——它用288兆像素的近红外相机，一次性覆盖超过满月面积200倍的天空。</p>

    <div class="bilingual-block en">

        <div class="lang-label">English</div>

        <p>Right now, on August 30, 2026, the Roman Space Telescope has officially lifted off aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket. If Hubble is a "precision microscope" and Webb is a "super-telephoto lens," then Roman is a "wide-angle panoramic camera"—its 288-megapixel near-infrared camera captures a single frame covering over 200 times the area of the full moon.</p>

    <div class="highlight-box">

        <h4>🔭 三大核心科学目标 · Three Core Science Goals</h4>

        <p><strong>1. 暗能量探测（Dark Energy）：</strong>通过精确测量数十亿颗星系的分布和形状，追溯宇宙加速膨胀的历史，揭示暗能量的本质。</p>

        <p><strong>1. Dark Energy:</strong> By precisely measuring the distribution and shapes of billions of galaxies, tracing the history of cosmic acceleration and revealing the nature of dark energy.</p>

        <p><strong>2. 暗物质映射（Dark Matter）：</strong>利用引力透镜效应，绘制宇宙中不可见暗物质的三维分布图，填补宇宙「暗影」的拼图。</p>

        <p><strong>2. Dark Matter:</strong> Using gravitational lensing to map the 3D distribution of invisible dark matter across the cosmos.</p>

        <p><strong>3. 系外行星搜寻（Exoplanets）：</strong>通过凌日法和微引力透镜法，发现并表征数百颗系外行星，包括潜在的岩石宜居世界。</p>

        <p><strong>3. Exoplanets:</strong> Through transit and microlensing methods, discovering and characterizing hundreds of exoplanets, including potentially rocky habitable worlds.</p>

    </div>



    <div class="bilingual-block cn">

        <div class="lang-label">中文</div>

        <p>罗曼的科学巡天效率是哈勃的数百倍。它每天产生的数据量高达11太字节，整个任务期间将积累约20 PB的数据——相当于全球互联网每天流量的数倍。它的广域巡天将在10年内覆盖超过5000平方度的天区，绘制一张跨越宇宙100亿年历史的三维星系地图。</p>

    </div>

    <div class="bilingual-block en">

        <div class="lang-label">English</div>

        <p>Roman's survey efficiency is hundreds of times that of Hubble. It generates up to 11 terabytes of data per day, accumulating approximately 20 petabytes over its mission—a volume several times the world's daily internet traffic. Its Wide Field Survey will cover over 5,000 square degrees of sky in 10 years, producing a three-dimensional map of galaxies spanning 10 billion years of cosmic history.</p>
