# 星际基地


STARBASE — Humanity's New Gateway to Deep Space


从德克萨斯州的博卡奇卡到路易斯安那州的佩坎岛，SpaceX正在建造人类历史上最大、最雄心勃勃的航天港——一个通往火星乃至更遥远深空的起点。

## 一、什么是星际基地What Is Starbase?*




星际基地（Starbase）是SpaceX公司建立的航天港与火箭研发制造中心，目前有两个地点：位于德克萨斯州博卡奇卡的原始基地，以及2026年8月正式公布的位于路易斯安那州佩坎岛的超级基地。两者共同构成了SpaceX多基地运营战略的核心。


Starbase is SpaceX's spaceport and rocket manufacturing complex, currently operating at two locations: the original base in **Boca Chica, Texas**, and the newly announced super base at **Pecan Island, Louisiana**, revealed in August 2026. Together, they form the core of SpaceX's multi-site operations strategy.






"Starbase"这个名字本身便充满象征意味——它既是字面意义上的"星际出发基地"，也呼应了人类向太空进发的终极愿景。马斯克在路易斯安那发布会现场表示："我们还没有给它起正式名字。""We haven't picked a name yet."——而台下观众喊出的"Starbase"当场被采纳。


The name "Starbase" itself is highly symbolic—it literally means a "star departure base" and echoes humanity's ultimate vision of venturing into space. At the Louisiana announcement, Musk said, "We haven't picked a name yet"—and the crowd's spontaneous chant of "Starbase" was adopted on the spot.

## 二、两大星际基地Two Starbases

### 德州Starbase：创始之地Texas Starbase: The Birthplace




德克萨斯州Starbase位于博卡奇卡（Boca Chica），靠近墨西哥边境，自2014年起被SpaceX选中作为星舰（Starship）的研发与试验场。这里是星舰原型机从设计到试飞的完整链条所在地——工厂、测试台架、发射塔、指挥中心一应俱全。它是全球第一个专为轨道级商业发射设计的航天港之一。


Texas Starbase is located in Boca Chica, near the Mexican border, and has served as SpaceX's Starship development and test site since 2014. It houses the full chain from prototype design to flight testing—factories, test stands, launch towers, and command centers. **It is one of the world's first commercial spaceports designed specifically for orbital-class launches.**






德克萨斯基地目前的定位是**研发与原型测试中心**，承担了星舰从SN系列到 orbital-class 原型的全部迭代工作。2023年4月，星舰首次完整级的静态点火试车在此完成；2024年3月，星舰第四次试飞成功入轨。


Texas Starbase currently serves as the **R&amp;D and prototype testing center**, handling all iterations of Starship from the SN series to orbital-class vehicles. In April 2023, the full-stack static fire test was completed here; in March 2024, Starship's fourth test flight achieved orbit successfully.

### 路易斯安那Starbase：未来巨港Louisiana Starbase: The Mega Port of the Future




2026年8月25日，SpaceX与路易斯安那州政府正式宣布，将在佩坎岛（Pecan Island，维尔米恩教区）附近建设**"Starbase Louisiana"**，占地约12.5万英亩（约506平方公里），预计投资**1000亿美元**。如果按规划全部建成，这将成为SpaceX在美国的**第四个也是最大的发射场**，超越德克萨斯基地的规模。


On August 25, 2026, SpaceX and the state of Louisiana formally announced the construction of **"Starbase Louisiana"** near Pecan Island (Vermilion Parish), covering approximately 125,000 acres (about 506 sq km), with an estimated investment of **$100 billion**. If fully completed as planned, it will become SpaceX's **fourth and largest launch site** in the United States, surpassing the Texas base in scale.






路易斯安那基地的规划蓝图极为宏大：十座以上发射工位、每天30次星舰发射能力、自建推进剂生产设施、深水港口、机场、电力生成系统。这是一个完全自给自足的航天港——从火箭燃料的制造到航天员的往返交通，一切都在基地内部闭环。


The master plan for Louisiana Starbase is extraordinarily ambitious: **over ten launch pads, the capacity for 30 Starship launches per day, self-built propellant production facilities, a deep-water port, an airport, and power generation systems**. This is a fully self-sustaining spaceport—from rocket fuel manufacturing to astronaut transportation, everything operates in a closed loop within the facility.

## 四、星际基地的核心优势Core Advantages of Starbase





#### ① 全链条自主 / Full-Stack Autonomy


从火箭设计、零件制造、总装测试到发射运营，SpaceX在Starbase实现了垂直整合。传统航天港中，火箭制造商、发射服务商、载荷集成商往往分属不同公司，协调成本极高。Starbase将这些环节全部压缩在同一地理空间内，大幅降低了沟通成本和迭代周期。





#### ② 高频发射能力 / High-Frequency Launch Capability


路易斯安那基地规划10+座发射工位，目标日发射30次。这意味着平均每48分钟就有一枚星舰升空。相比之下，肯尼迪航天中心高峰期年发射约15次，平均间隔约24天。





#### ③ 推进剂自给 / Propellant Self-Sufficiency


星舰使用液氧+甲烷作为推进剂。路州基地规划在本地建设甲烷和液氧生产设施，这意味着每次发射的燃料供应不再依赖长途运输，大幅降低了发射成本和供应链风险。





#### ④ 深水港与物流 / Deep-Water Port & Logistics


路州基地规划深水港口，允许大型船只运输超尺度的火箭部件。星舰全长120米、直径9米，无法通过公路或铁路陆路运输。深水港使得海上运输成为可能，从西海岸制造厂直接运到发射场。





#### ⑤ 地理优势 / Geographic Advantages


佩坎岛位于墨西哥湾沿岸，纬度约29°N，与德州博卡奇卡（26°N）相近，低纬度意味着发射到赤道轨道时可以借助地球自转速度节省燃料。同时，向东发射时火箭残骸落入开阔海域，安全性高。





#### ⑥ 政策与税收激励 / Policy & Tax Incentives


路易斯安那州为吸引SpaceX投入，通过了专门的立法框架，提供土地、税收减免和监管简化。该投资被描述为该州历史上最大单笔资本投入，州政府有极强动力保障项目顺利推进。

## 五、技术特点与创新Technical Features & Innovations

### 星舰回收：著名的"塔捕"Starship Recovery: The Famous "Mechazilla" Catch




与传统火箭一级助推器通过海上平台伞降回收不同，星舰Super Heavy助推器的回收方式堪称革命性——利用发射塔上的机械臂在飞行中直接捕获助推器。这种"筷子夹火箭"的方式由马斯克命名为*"Mechazilla"*，它不仅大幅提升了回收可靠性，还使得发射工位可以实现快速周转，是支撑日发射30次目标的关键技术。


Unlike traditional rocket boosters that recover via parachute onto ocean platforms, Starship's Super Heavy booster recovery is revolutionary—using mechanical arms on the launch tower to catch the booster mid-flight. Elon Musk nicknamed this "chopstick-catch" method *"Mechazilla."* It significantly improves recovery reliability and enables rapid turnaround of launch pads, which is the key technology supporting the goal of 30 launches per day.



### 甲烷燃料路线Methane Fuel Architecture




星舰选择液氧+液态甲烷（LOX/LCH₄）作为推进剂组合。这一选择在航天史上具有深远意义：**甲烷燃烧后产生的积碳极少，可以显著减少发动机维护间隔**，实现快速复用。更重要的是，甲烷可以在火星上通过Sabatier反应从二氧化碳和地下水冰中合成，这意味着星舰在未来火星任务中可以实现"异地燃料生产"——为火星返回地球的任务奠定基础。


Starship's choice of liquid oxygen + liquid methane (LOX/LCH₄) as propellant is profoundly significant in aerospace history: **methane combustion produces minimal soot, dramatically reducing engine maintenance intervals and enabling rapid reuse.** More importantly, **methane can be synthesized on Mars via the Sabatier reaction from atmospheric CO₂ and subsurface water ice**—meaning Starship can achieve "in-situ propellant production" on Mars, laying the foundation for Mars-return missions.



### 完全可复用设计Fully Reusable Design




星舰是整个航天史上第一个设计目标是完全可复用的大型运载火箭——从 Super Heavy 助推器到 Starship 飞船本体，两级均可多次飞行。SpaceX的目标是将单次发射成本从传统火箭的每千克数万美元降至数百美元，这是实现大规模星际运输的经济基础。


Starship is the first large launch vehicle in aerospace history designed with the goal of **full reusability**—both the Super Heavy booster and the Starship spacecraft are designed for multiple flights. SpaceX aims to reduce per-kilogram launch costs from tens of thousands of dollars to hundreds of dollars, which forms the economic foundation for large-scale interplanetary transportation.

## 七、对人类未来的影响Impact on Humanity's Future

### 让火星殖民成为可能Making Mars Colonization Feasible




火星是人类太空探索的终极目标之一。但火星距离地球最近时也有约5,460万公里，单程飞行需要6-9个月。在此之前，最大的障碍不是工程技术，而是经济成本。当前火箭发射成本约为每千克1-2万美元（猎鹰9号），而星舰的目标是将其降至100美元以下。星际基地作为星舰的"家"，是这个成本革命得以实现的物理载体。


Mars is one of humanity's ultimate goals in space exploration. But even at its closest, Mars is about 54.6 million kilometers away, with a one-way journey taking 6-9 months. Before that, the biggest obstacle is not engineering but **economic cost**. Current rocket launch costs are about $10,000-20,000 per kilogram (Falcon 9), while Starship targets under $100/kg. Starbase, as Starship's "home," is the physical carrier enabling this cost revolution.



### 太空经济的引擎Engine of the Space Economy




星际基地不只是发射场，它是一个综合性太空产业生态：包括推进剂工厂、卫星组装车间、太空垃圾清理设施、轨道加油站等。按照SpaceX的规划，路州基地还将配套机场、港口、居住区，形成一个真正意义上的"太空城"。这将创造数万个工作岗位，并带动上下游产业链的发展。


Starbase is not just a launch site—it's a **comprehensive space industry ecosystem**: including propellant plants, satellite assembly workshops, space debris cleanup facilities, and orbital refueling stations. Per SpaceX's plan, the Louisiana base will also feature an airport, port, and residential areas, forming a true "space city." This will create tens of thousands of jobs and drive the development of upstream and downstream industries.



### 地球能源与气候的潜在贡献Potential Contributions to Earth's Energy & Climate




一个常被忽视的维度是：星际基地支持的**轨道太阳能电站（SBSP）**构想。如果在太空建设大型太阳能阵列，24小时不间断地将能量以微波形式传回地球，将彻底改变人类的能源结构。星际基地的低成本发射能力，是让这一构想起步的必要条件。


An often-overlooked dimension is the **Space-Based Solar Power (SBSP)** concept supported by Starbase. If large solar arrays were built in space, continuously beaming energy back to Earth via microwave, it would fundamentally transform human energy infrastructure. Starbase's low-cost launch capability is the necessary precondition for initiating such concepts.



### 多行星文明的起点The Starting Point of a Multi-Planetary Civilization




马斯克多次公开表示，建立星际基地的最终目的是让人类成为多行星物种。当地球面临小行星撞击、核战争、气候灾难等存在性风险时，拥有一个独立的火星文明备份，是整个人类种族层面的保险策略。星际基地正是这一宏大愿景的物理起点——没有低成本、高频次的发射能力，多行星文明就只是空想。


Musk has repeatedly stated publicly that the ultimate purpose of building Starbase is to make humanity a **multi-planetary species**. When Earth faces existential risks—asteroid impacts, nuclear war, climate disasters—having an independent Mars civilization backup is an insurance strategy at the species level. Starbase is the physical starting point of this grand vision; without low-cost, high-frequency launch capability, a multi-planetary civilization remains a fantasy.

## 八、结语Conclusion




从德克萨斯州博卡奇卡的一片滩涂，到路易斯安那州佩坎岛的十万英亩湿地，星际基地的故事是一个关于野心、工程与现实的故事。它既是对传统航天工业模式的颠覆——从政府主导的多用户共享设施转向私营企业的垂直整合，也是对人类太空探索边界的拓展——从近地轨道走向深空。


From a beachfront in Boca Chica, Texas to 125,000 acres of wetland at Pecan Island, Louisiana, the story of Starbase is one about **ambition, engineering, and reality**. It is both a subversion of the traditional aerospace industrial model—from government-led multi-user shared facilities to private enterprise vertical integration—and an expansion of humanity's space exploration boundaries—from low Earth orbit to deep space.






星际基地不是终点，而是起点。它的真正意义不在于某一天有多少枚火箭升空，而在于它证明了：当一家私营企业以数十年为尺度规划太空基础设施时，曾经只属于国家的太空能力，正在被重新定义。人类向星际时代迈出的第一步，或许正从路易斯安那州的沼泽地中悄然孕育。


Starbase is not the destination—it is the starting point. Its true significance does not lie in how many rockets launch on any given day, but in what it proves: when a private enterprise plans space infrastructure on a decades-long timescale, space capabilities once exclusive to nations are being redefined. **The first steps of humanity into the interstellar age may be quietly taking root in the marshlands of Louisiana.**
