中文 / Chinese

        今天，美国佛罗里达州肯尼迪航天中心，一声轰鸣划破长空。SpaceX猎鹰重型火箭搭载着NASA的南希·格蕾丝·罗曼太空望远镜，于美东时间上午7:26（北京时间19:26）成功升空。这是人类迄今最先进的红外空间望远镜之一，它将开启一个探测暗能量、暗物质与系外行星的新时代。让我们从哈勃谈起，回溯这条深邃的探索之路。

    

    

        
English

        Today, a thunderous roar split the sky at NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. The Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, carried by a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket, lifted off successfully at 7:26 AM ET (7:26 PM Beijing time). As one of the most advanced infrared space telescopes humanity has ever built, it ushers in a new era of exploring dark energy, dark matter, and exoplanets. Let us begin with Hubble and retrace this profound journey of exploration.

    

    
    ## 一、哈勃：那只开启新纪元的眼睛 I. Hubble: The Eye That Opened a New Era

    

        
中文

        1990年4月24日，发现号航天飞机将哈勃空间望远镜送入约547公里高的地球轨道。那一刻，人类第一次将一颗永不闭合的眼睛，永久地投向深空。

    

    

        
English

        On April 24, 1990, the Space Shuttle *Discovery* deployed the Hubble Space Telescope into an Earth orbit approximately 547 kilometers high. In that moment, humanity placed for the first time an unblinking eye—permanently pointed toward the deep cosmos.

    

    

        

![插图1](/images/nasa/nasa-illustration-1.svg)

        
图：哈勃空间望远镜在地球轨道上运行，其2.4米主镜已捕捉超过160万张深空图像。
Illustration: Hubble in Earth orbit, its 2.4m mirror having captured over 1.6 million deep-sky images.

    

    

        
中文

        哈勃最震撼人类的成果，莫过于1995年发布的「哈勃深场」。那只是一块看似空无一物的微小天区，曝光整整10天后，数千个遥远星系赫然显现——有些来自130亿年前。它证明了一个朴素却深刻的事实：越远的天体，我们看到的就是越早的宇宙。

    

    

        
English

        Hubble's most breathtaking achievement was the 1995 release of the **Hubble Deep Field**. It pointed at what appeared to be an utterly empty patch of sky for 10 consecutive days. When the images were stitched together, thousands of distant galaxies emerged—some dating back 13 billion years. It proved a simple yet profound truth: the farther we look, the further back in time we see.

    

    

        
中文

        哈勃的另一项革命性贡献，是帮助科学家确认宇宙正在加速膨胀，从而推断出「暗能量」的存在。这项发现荣获2011年诺贝尔物理学奖。自1990年以来，哈勃已为人类贡献超过160万次观测，催生了近2万篇科学论文。

    

    

        
English

        Hubble's other revolutionary contribution was helping scientists confirm that the universe's expansion is accelerating, leading to the inference of **dark energy**—a discovery that earned the 2011 Nobel Prize in Physics. Since 1990, Hubble has completed over 1.6 million observations and spawned nearly 20,000 scientific papers.

    

    
    ## 二、韦伯：接棒后，看得更远 II. Webb: Receiving the Torch, Seeing Farther

    

        
中文

        哈勃的主要缺点是观测波长偏短——它最擅长可见光和紫外，对遥远星系因宇宙膨胀而红移的光线无能为力。为此，NASA启动了「下一代太空望远镜」项目，后命名为詹姆斯·韦伯空间望远镜。

    

    

        
English

        Hubble's key limitation was its wavelength range—it excelled in visible and ultraviolet light but was powerless against the redshifted light from distant galaxies, stretched by cosmic expansion. To address this, NASA launched the **Next Generation Space Telescope** project, later named the James Webb Space Telescope.

    

    

        

![插图2](/images/nasa/nasa-illustration-2.svg)

        
图：韦伯望远镜的18面六边形金铍镜段组成6.5米主镜，五层遮阳板将其冷却至-233°C以下，使其能在红外波段俯瞰宇宙诞生之初。
Illustration: Webb's 18 hexagonal gold-beryllium mirror segments form a 6.5m primary mirror; its five-layer sunshield cools it below -233°C for infrared observation.

    

    

        
中文

        2021年12月25日，韦伯随阿丽亚娜5号火箭从法属圭亚那发射升空，经过5个月的精准旅程抵达日地L₂拉格朗日点——距离地球约150万公里。它的18面六边形铍镜拼成一面直径6.5米的主镜，反射面镀金，能捕捉近乎完全被红移到红外线波段的早期宇宙光线。

    

    

        
English

        On December 25, 2021, Webb launched aboard an Ariane 5 rocket from French Guiana. After a precise five-month journey, it arrived at the Sun-Earth L₂ Lagrange point—about 1.5 million kilometers from Earth. Its 18 hexagonal beryllium segments form a 6.5-meter gold-coated primary mirror, capable of capturing early-universe light almost entirely redshifted into the infrared.

    

    

        
中文

        2022年7月，韦伯公布了首批全彩图像，立刻震惊世界。SMACS 0723星系团深处的「韦伯最深红外场」中，数千个星系如钻石般闪烁，其中一些形成于宇宙大爆炸后仅数亿年。韦伯甚至在一颗系外行星的大气中检测到了二氧化碳的存在。

    

    

        
English

        In July 2022, Webb released its first full-color images, stunning the world. In the "Webb's Deepest Infrared Field" of galaxy cluster SMACS 0723, thousands of galaxies shimmered like diamonds—some formed merely hundreds of millions of years after the Big Bang. Webb even detected carbon dioxide in the atmosphere of an exoplanet.

    

    
    ## 三、罗曼：更大视野，更深秘密 III. Roman: Wider View, Deeper Secrets

    

        
中文

        就在今天，2026年8月30日，罗曼太空望远镜搭乘猎鹰重型火箭正式升空。如果说哈勃是「精密的显微镜」，韦伯是「超远望远镜」，那罗曼就是「广角全景相机」——它用288兆像素的近红外相机，一次性覆盖超过满月面积200倍的天空。

    

    

        
English

        Right now, on August 30, 2026, the Roman Space Telescope has officially lifted off aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket. If Hubble is a "precision microscope" and Webb is a "super-telephoto lens," then Roman is a "wide-angle panoramic camera"—its 288-megapixel near-infrared camera captures a single frame covering over 200 times the area of the full moon.

    

    

        

![插图3](/images/nasa/nasa-illustration-3.svg)

        
图：罗曼望远镜的广域巡天单次拍摄面积相当于200个哈勃视场，能在10年内绘制跨越100亿年历史的三维宇宙地图。
Illustration: Roman's wide-field survey covers 200× Hubble's field per image, mapping a 3D universe spanning 10 billion years over its 10-year mission.

    

    

        #### 🔭 三大核心科学目标 · Three Core Science Goals

        **1. 暗能量探测（Dark Energy）：**通过精确测量数十亿颗星系的分布和形状，追溯宇宙加速膨胀的历史，揭示暗能量的本质。

        **1. Dark Energy:** By precisely measuring the distribution and shapes of billions of galaxies, tracing the history of cosmic acceleration and revealing the nature of dark energy.

        **2. 暗物质映射（Dark Matter）：**利用引力透镜效应，绘制宇宙中不可见暗物质的三维分布图，填补宇宙「暗影」的拼图。

        **2. Dark Matter:** Using gravitational lensing to map the 3D distribution of invisible dark matter across the cosmos.

        **3. 系外行星搜寻（Exoplanets）：**通过凌日法和微引力透镜法，发现并表征数百颗系外行星，包括潜在的岩石宜居世界。

        **3. Exoplanets:** Through transit and microlensing methods, discovering and characterizing hundreds of exoplanets, including potentially rocky habitable worlds.

    

    

        
中文

        罗曼的科学巡天效率是哈勃的数百倍。它每天产生的数据量高达11太字节，整个任务期间将积累约20 PB的数据——相当于全球互联网每天流量的数倍。它的广域巡天将在10年内覆盖超过5000平方度的天区，绘制一张跨越宇宙100亿年历史的三维星系地图。

    

    

        
English

        Roman's survey efficiency is hundreds of times that of Hubble. It generates up to 11 terabytes of data per day, accumulating approximately 20 petabytes over its mission—a volume several times the world's daily internet traffic. Its Wide Field Survey will cover over 5,000 square degrees of sky in 10 years, producing a three-dimensional map of galaxies spanning 10 billion years of cosmic history.

    

    
    ## 四、深空探索的接力赛 IV. The Relay Race of Deep-Space Exploration

    

        
中文

        从哈勃到韦伯再到罗曼，这不是一条单线演进，而是一场精密设计的接力赛。每一代望远镜都以前一代的局限为起点，又为下一代指明方向。

    

    

        
English

        From Hubble to Webb to Roman—this is not a linear progression but a meticulously choreographed relay race. Each generation of telescope begins where the previous one left off, while pointing the way for the next.

    

    

        

            

            
1990

            
哈勃空间望远镜升空 · Hubble Launch

            
2.4米主镜，可见光/紫外观测。发布「哈勃深场」，精确测定宇宙膨胀速率，发现星系中心普遍存在超大质量黑洞。
2.4m mirror, visible/UV observation. Published the Hubble Deep Field, precisely measured the universe's expansion rate, discovered supermassive black holes at galaxy centers.

        

        

            

            
2009

            
普朗克卫星升空 · Planck Satellite

            
精确测绘宇宙微波背景辐射，将暗能量占宇宙总能量的比例锁定在68.3%。
Precisely mapped the Cosmic Microwave Background, locking dark energy at 68.3% of total cosmic energy.

        

        

            

            
2021

            
韦伯空间望远镜升空 · Webb Launch

            
6.5米金铍主镜，红外观测。捕捉宇宙最早星系，分析系外行星大气成分，开启宇宙黎明时代。
6.5m gold-beryllium mirror, infrared observation. Captured the earliest galaxies and analyzed exoplanet atmospheres, opening the cosmic dawn era.

        

        

            

            
2026 · 今天

            
罗曼太空望远镜升空 · Roman Launch (Today)

            
2.4米主镜 + 288兆像素广角相机，巡天效率碾压前代。聚焦暗能量、暗物质与系外行星三大终极谜题。
2.4m mirror + 288MP wide-field camera, survey efficiency far surpassing predecessors. Focused on three终极 mysteries: dark energy, dark matter, and exoplanets.

    ## 五、为什么这些望远镜如此重要？ V. Why Do These Telescopes Matter?

    

        
中文

        深空望远镜的价值，远不止于拍出漂亮的照片。它们是人类回答最根本问题的工具。

    

    

        
English

        The value of deep-space telescopes extends far beyond pretty pictures. They are humanity's tools for answering the most fundamental questions.

    

    ### 宇宙在如何演化？ How is the Universe Evolving?

    

        
中文

        哈勃和罗曼通过观测不同时代的星系，构建了一部「宇宙成长史」。我们借此了解到，星系并非一成不变，而是经历了从混沌到有序、从小到大的漫长演化。

    

    

        
English

        Hubble and Roman, by observing galaxies across different epochs, have constructed a "cosmic growth story." We have learned that galaxies are not static—they evolved from chaotic, small proto-galaxies into the ordered, massive structures we see today.

    

    ### 我们在宇宙中孤独吗？ Are We Alone in the Cosmos?

    

        
中文

        韦伯已在系外行星大气中检测到二氧化碳、水蒸气等分子；罗曼则将在统计层面揭示数千颗系外行星的性质——包括岩石质的类地行星。

    

    

        
English

        Webb has already detected molecules like carbon dioxide and water vapor in exoplanet atmospheres; Roman will reveal the properties of thousands of exoplanets statistically—including rocky, Earth-like worlds.

    

    ### 宇宙的「隐形骨架」是什么？ What is the Universe's Invisible Skeleton?

    

        
中文

        我们可见的普通物质只占宇宙总能量的5%，其余95%是暗物质（27%）和暗能量（68%）。罗曼将通过引力透镜效应，以前所未有的精度绘制暗物质分布图。

    

    

        
English

        Visible ordinary matter accounts for only 5% of the universe's total energy; the remaining 95% is dark matter (27%) and dark energy (68%). Roman will map dark matter distribution with unprecedented precision using gravitational lensing.

        #### 💡 一个令人震撼的对比 · A Striking Comparison

        **中文：**哈勃深场拍摄的那一小块天空，面积仅相当于从地面看一枚硬币在100米外的视角。然而其中包含了约**5500个星系**，总数超过可观测宇宙中所有星系数量的十分之一。

        **English:** The tiny patch of sky captured in the Hubble Deep Field is no larger than a coin viewed from 100 meters away. Yet it contains approximately **5,500 galaxies**—more than one-tenth of all galaxies observable in the entire universe.

        **中文：**罗曼的广角相机在一次拍摄中就能覆盖相当于**400个哈勃深场**的面积。它的巡天计划，将首次让人类以统计学的规模，系统性地探索暗能量的本质。

        **English:** Roman's wide-field camera can cover an area equivalent to **400 Hubble Deep Fields** in a single exposure. Its survey program will allow humanity, for the first time, to systematically explore the nature of dark energy on a statistical scale.

    

    
    ## 六、结语：仰望，从未停止 VI. Conclusion: We Never Stop Looking Up

    

        
中文

        从伽利略1609年第一次将望远镜对准星空，到今天罗曼升空、韦伯运转、哈勃仍在服役，人类看向宇宙的目光从未停歇。每一次技术的跃升，都打开了一个全新的窗口——我们看到过更早的星系、更远的超新星、更近的世界。

    

    

        
English

        From Galileo's first pointing of a telescope at the stars in 1609, to today's launch of Roman, the运转 of Webb, and Hubble still in service—humanity's gaze toward the cosmos has never rested. Each technological leap has opened an entirely new window: we have seen earlier galaxies, more distant supernovae, and nearer worlds.

    

    

        
中文

        罗曼不是终点，而是又一个起跑线。当它明年年初传回第一批图像时，或许会再次颠覆我们今天的认知——就像1995年的哈勃深场一样。宇宙的谜题远未解开，而人类的眼睛，只会越擦越亮。

    

    

        
English

        Roman is not the end but another starting line. When it returns its first images in early next year, it may once again overturn our current understanding—just as the Hubble Deep Field did in 1995. The universe's puzzles remain far from solved, and humanity's eyes will only grow keener.
